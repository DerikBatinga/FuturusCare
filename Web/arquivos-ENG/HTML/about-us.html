Our History

We were born out of unconditional love for animals and the desire to provide
peace of mind for families who need to be away.

how it all began

FuturusCare was founded in 2025, when our team realized that many pet owners were struggling to find reliable caregivers for their pets during trips or periods of absence.

As professionals who are passionate about animals, we decided to create a service that combines professional expertise with the affection of a family. Today, we already care for more 500 pets in São Paulo and the surrounding area.

ur mission is simple: to ensure that your pet receives all the love, care, and attention it deserves, so that you can have complete peace of mind knowing that it is in good hands.
